EDC for Your Relationship

You carry tools for physical obstacles. What do you carry for the emotional ones? The Partner Pact is a tactical communication deck designed to cut through the noise, clear the fog of war, and solve conflicts before they start.

No therapy. No fluff. Just essential gear.

Available in three language editions: English · Spanish · Czech

01 · The Fog of War

Most Arguments Are Just Bad Signal Processing

You are a team. You love each other. Yet, you find yourselves stuck in loops of misunderstanding. You say: "I need space to think." She hears: "I’m rejecting you."

Assumptions pile up. The “Fog of War” sets in. Suddenly, you aren't solving the problem - you are fighting each other.

It’s not a lack of love. It’s a lack of the right interface. You don't need to change who you are. You just need better tools to translate your needs into clear signals.

02 · The Protocol

A Tactical Interface for Two

I approached this problem the same way I approach survival or coding: Strip away the unnecessary. Focus on function. Build what lasts.

Think of The Partner Pact as your Relational EDC (Everyday Carry). It turns invisible, complex feelings into visible, tangible objects.

  • Overwhelmed? Play the SANCTUARY card. It’s not an excuse; it’s a break you need to re-charge and re-consider. It signals a need for a tactical retreat to recharge, ensuring you return as a better partner.
  • Need backup? Play the QUEST card. Transform a chore into a shared mission.
  • Lost direction? Summon the WAR COUNCIL. Stop arguing and start strategizing your future together.

Deployment

Deployment in 3 Steps

1. Fabricate the System (The Guide)

First, download The Guide. It contains the blueprints and assembly instructions. Follow them to print and cut two full sets of The Deck - one equipment set for you, and one for your partner.

2. Initialize the Alliance (The Pact)

Sit down together and read The Pact. Sign it to officially shift your dynamic from “opponents” to “allies.” This ritual confirms you are now facing challenges together, not against each other.

3. Engage the Protocol (The Deck)

Start the action. Don’t overthink words or guess feelings - simply play a card from your deck. Whether you need a pause, a hug, or support, the card signals your needs instantly without the drama.

Forged by Experience

The Partner Pact

I think, plan and build. I enjoy strategic board games, history, SF&F and the principles of survival - where preparation, experience and good allies can make all the difference. I like solving problems, stripping away the unnecessary, focusing on what matters and turning ideas into practical, working systems.

The Partner Pact started with a problem I encountered in my own marriage. We didn’t need a therapist asking us “how that made us feel.” We needed clarity. We needed a better system. I brought together logic and design, a passion for board games and a tactical mindset to create something tangible - something we could actually hold in our hands. Inspired by ancient codes of honor and modern tactical aesthetics, I created The Partner Pact - a deck of cards that turns everyday conflicts into missions and adventures we both enjoy.

First, I tried out and tested the idea at home. Then, drawing on my experience with web development, programming, AI and a just build it approach, I turned it into something others could use, too.

The result is visually appealing, simple to use and - most importantly - fun to play. :)
